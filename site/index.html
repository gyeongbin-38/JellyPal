WHAT IS IT?

YOUR DESKTOP BUDDY

A little pixel slime lives on your screen. It naps on your windows, follows your cursor, and never gets in the way of your work. Click it to pet it. Toss it to watch it squish.

IT EATS KEYSTROKES

Typing feeds your pal. Every 600 keystrokes it spits out a jelly — the currency it uses to pull new species, buy snacks and decorate its little ranch.

ALWAYS DOING SOMETHING

Minimize it and it keeps living — wandering, snacking, napping. Come back after lunch and collect the jelly it earned while you were gone.

COLLECT & BREED

48+ SPECIES

Round ones, flat ones, tall ones, puddle ones. Each species has its own personality, movement style and signature trick. Four rarities, plus rare shiny variants.

HYBRID BREEDING

Pair two pals and the egg mixes their shapes, colors, traits and skills into a hybrid nobody else has. Babies grow up in the nursery while you watch.

LEGENDARY PALS

Eleven legendaries with screen-filling signature moves — starbursts, gravity wells, royal decrees — and passive auras that change how your ranch behaves.

A LITTLE RANCH

Food bowls, cushions, a music box, a cookie jar. Up to three companion slimes wander your screen and actually use everything you place.

FAQ

WINDOWS SMARTSCREEN WARNS ME?

Totally normal for a small unsigned app — click More info → Run anyway. Jellypal is a plain local app: no ads, no tracking, no account needed.

INSTALLER OR ZIP?

The installer (setup.exe) is the easy path — it installs to C:\Jellypal and bundles the WebView2 runtime. The portable zip runs from anywhere but needs Edge WebView2 already on your PC (most Windows 11 machines have it).

WILL IT SLOW MY PC?

No — it's a 5 MB app that sips CPU. It's designed for laptops and small machines.

MAC?

A macOS build is in the works. Windows first.
