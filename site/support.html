Get your slime unstuck.

Most Jellypal problems are local and reversible. Start with the quick checks below before reinstalling or resetting anything — the reset really does erase the collection on this device.

Quick checks first

  1. Wait ten seconds after launch. The first frame can take a moment on a busy Mac.
  2. Click the slime once. If it reacts, the renderer is alive and the issue is usually a panel or window position.
  3. Open the menu with the round button, then close and reopen the panel you were using.
  4. Quit from Settings → QUIT or the menu bar, then launch Jellypal again.

If the app does not appear at all, check whether it is hidden behind another window or on another desktop space. The companion window can stay above ordinary windows, but macOS full-screen spaces are separate.

Jelly purchases

Jelly packs are optional consumable in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment; Jellypal never sees your card number or Apple ID password.

If a purchase is interrupted, keep Jellypal open and launch it again while signed in to the same Mac App Store account. StoreKit can redeliver an unfinished transaction, and the app credits jelly before it finishes that transaction.

Consumables are not restored after a reset, reinstall, or move to a different Mac. Apple does not treat consumed jelly as a restorable purchase. The saved jelly balance lives with the local save.

For a missing payment, duplicate charge, cancellation, or refund decision, use Apple's purchase-support flow first. Apple owns the payment record.

Windows and panels

  • Drag an empty cream-colored part of the companion window to move it.
  • The Ranch, Nursery, and settings panels scroll vertically when the collection is taller than the display.
  • On small or scaled displays, the app shrinks panels to fit the usable work area. If a panel still opens in an awkward place, close it, resize the display arrangement, and open it again.
  • If a display is disconnected, reopening the app recenters saved panel positions instead of leaving them off-screen.

Save and reset

Your species, hybrids, jelly, names, accessories, room props, photos, and settings are stored locally on the Mac. There is no Jellypal cloud account.

Settings → RESET → SURE? permanently wipes the local save and starts over. Do not use it as a troubleshooting step unless you are willing to lose the collection.

Privacy and permissions

The Mac App Store version does not use Accessibility, Input Monitoring, or Screen Recording permissions. It does not send analytics, keystrokes, save data, screen contents, or usage tracking to us.

Read the full privacy policy before deleting app data or sharing diagnostics.

Contact the developer

Use the public Jellypal support issue tracker for bug reports, feature requests, and purchase troubleshooting that does not include private information. Do not post your save file, Apple ID, receipt, or payment details.

When reporting a bug, include your macOS version, whether you use Apple silicon or Intel, what you clicked, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot of the companion window is usually enough.
