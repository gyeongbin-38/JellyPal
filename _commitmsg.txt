make pack checkout links provider-agnostic

BUY buttons already link out to hosted checkout, but the uid passthrough
was Stripe-only (client_reference_id on buy.stripe.com). Now any https
checkout link works: stripe keeps client_reference_id, paddle links get
custom_data[uid], anything else gets a harmless ?uid=. Site pack buttons
accept any provider's link too instead of requiring the stripe domain.
